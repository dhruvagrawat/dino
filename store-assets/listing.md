## Full description (Play, ≤4000 chars)
```
Run, jump, and survive in Dino — a polished take on the classic dino runner, rebuilt with modern pixel-art style, satisfying haptics, and tons to unlock. And it works 100% offline.

Tap to jump, hold to duck, and dodge an endless desert of cacti, birds, rocks, and even meteors. The longer you last, the faster it gets — how far can you go?

🦖 THREE DINOS TO UNLOCK
• Rex — the balanced original
• Raptor — tap again mid-air for a double jump
• Tank — armored and tough, survives one hit per run

🎨 FIVE COLLECTIBLE SKINS
Classic, Neon, Lava, Ice and Gold — spend the points you earn to deck out your runner.

🌗 THREE GAME MODES
• Classic — the endless desert with a day/night cycle
• Frenzy — blazing speed and tighter gaps, with 2× points
• Moon — low gravity and falling meteors under a starry sky, with 1.5× points

⭐ EARN AND UNLOCK
Every run earns points. Spend them to unlock new dinos, skins, and modes. Your scores and progress are saved right on your device.

📴 TRULY OFFLINE & PRIVATE
No internet required. No ads. No accounts. No data collected — ever. Everything stays on your phone.

⚡ MADE TO FEEL GOOD
Smooth pixel-art animation, responsive controls, haptic feedback, and a clean, friendly design.

Free to play. No in-app purchases. No tracking. Just a fun runner that respects you.

Made with ♥ by Quadcydle.
```
