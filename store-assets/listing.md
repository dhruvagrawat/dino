## Full description (Play, ≤4000 chars)
```
Suit up as Voltbot and race across a glowing synthwave city! Voltbot is a fast, juicy pixel-art endless runner packed with power-ups, combos and unlockables, and it works 100% offline.

Tap to hop, hold to jump higher, and duck under patrol drones. Leap traffic cones and crate stacks, grab glowing energy cells and skim past danger for close-call bonuses. The longer you survive, the faster it gets. How far can you go?

🤖 FOUR BOTS TO UNLOCK
• Volt: the balanced original
• Zip: jet boots for a mid-air double jump
• Brick: heavy plating that survives one hit per run
• Nova: a built-in magnet that pulls in nearby energy cells

⚡ POWER-UPS & COMBOS
• Magnet pulls every cell your way
• Shield absorbs a crash
• 2× Score doubles your distance points
• Chain close calls for a growing bonus multiplier

🌆 THREE NEON WORLDS
• Neon City: rooftops that fade from day into a glowing neon night
• Overdrive: a sunset highway at blazing speed, with 2× points
• Orbit: low gravity and flying meteors in deep space, with 1.5× points

🎨 FIVE PAINT JOBS
Core, Toxic, Sunset, Frost and Gold. Spend the points you earn to customise your bot.

📴 TRULY OFFLINE & PRIVATE
No internet required. No ads. No accounts. No data collected, ever. Everything stays on your phone.

✨ MADE TO FEEL GOOD
Responsive controls, particle effects, screen shake, parallax scenery and haptic feedback.

Free to play. No in-app purchases. No tracking. Just a fun runner that respects you.

Made with ♥ by Quadcydle.
```
